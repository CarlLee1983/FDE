Before technology

Remove repeated work and standardize the control boundary

  1. 1
    Eliminate repeated lookup

    Assemble one permitted read-only evidence bundle before the representative interprets status.

  2. 2
    Standardize meaning

    Publish one finance-owned semantic version for the three supported normalized states.

  3. 3
    Move controls forward

    Check record presence, consistency, freshness, dispute status, and access before presenting a status.

  4. 4
    Clarify ownership

    Customer service owns the normal inquiry; finance owns every unsupported or unsafe state.

  5. 5
    Keep judgment human

    The representative confirms the response; the view never answers the customer directly.

Technology-neutral target

Retrieve, control, normalize, then let a human confirm

  1. 01Retrieve

    Resolve permitted order and payment evidence with source identity and retrieval time.

    Deterministic software
  2. 02Control

    Evaluate record presence, consistency, freshness, dispute status, and access.

    Deterministic software
  3. 03Normalize

    Map only one of the three supported states using finance-owned versioned rules.

    Deterministic software
  4. 04Confirm

    Review the evidence-bearing summary and confirm or reject the response template.

    Customer-service representative
  5. 05Escalate

    Resolve every abstained, disputed, failed, rejected, stale, conflicting, missing, or unauthorized case.

    Finance refund owner

Normal: A human-confirmed response based on a supported normalized state.

Exception: An explicit abstention with a finance escalation reason.

Override: A representative may reject the template and escalate, but cannot alter the normalized state or policy.

AI decision

AI-fit: not needed for the first slice

Retrieval, state mapping, access checks, exception rules, and response templates are deterministic. Adding AI would not remove the diagnosed bottleneck.

Evaluate AI later only for measurable language or wording value after the deterministic shadow baseline is accepted; AI may not map states, fill missing evidence, answer customers directly, or authorize an action.
