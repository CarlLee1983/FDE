# FDE Capability

This context defines the role and reusable operating capabilities that the project is intended to strengthen.

## Language

**FDE (Forward Deployed Engineer)**:
An engineer embedded in a concrete business setting who translates operating problems into working, governed capabilities.
_Avoid_: alternative acronyms or expanding FDE as an engineering discipline

**FDE capability**:
A governed operating capability that an FDE can deliver, validate, reuse, and evolve across business scenarios.
_Avoid_: isolated demo, one-off AI answer
